# Safe academic certificate integration

## Why
Integrate the UNCSM academic processes using the existing data model and real permissions.

## What Changes
Existing issued documents can be consulted and publicly verified with minimal fields; unavailable official templates and revocation policy are recorded as blockers rather than fabricated.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `ecosystem`: Safe academic certificate integration.

## Impact
Local API, backoffice and student portal as applicable. No production mutations or deployments.
