# Academic billing and administrative usability

## Why
Administrators need reachable form actions and retained edits. Caja and students need authoritative balances derived from accepted obligations rather than manually entered prices.

## What Changes
Improve existing program/cohort forms and institutional styles. Introduce exact-money cohort payment plans, immutable enrollment obligations, transactional payment allocation and internal receipts. Connect cohort configuration, cashier operations, authorized dashboards and student accounts. Preserve documentary/quorum rules and existing permissions. Matriculation activation requires an explicit administrator decision, including free cohorts, as confirmed by the user.

## Impact
POSGRADOSETECAPI is canonical; setecweb and setecportal consume it. Local implementation only. No production data, remote operations, banking integrations or fiscal-compliance claims. Full scope and evidence: odd/tasks/academic-billing-ux.md.
